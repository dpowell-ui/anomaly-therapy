Questions

Before you enroll.

I'm not fully licensed yet. Is this for me?

Yes. Most attendees will be post-master's therapists or associate therapists. We cover what that allows, how supervision shapes your choices, and what you can set up now.

Does this cover insurance billing?

No. The seminar focuses on private-pay practice, which is how Anomaly Therapy is built and what Jess can speak to from experience.

What if I can't attend live?

Every enrollment includes the recording and the workbook.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. You'll get a clear map and the right questions. Entity choice, S-corp elections and tax filings should be confirmed with a Tennessee attorney or CPA.

Is there more after this?

Yes. This seminar covers the logistics. The next training, How I built a private practice with a team of five in five years, covers the story behind them: the mistakes, the investment, the boundaries and the numbers. Attendees get the first invitation.

Why does the price go up?

The founding price is for students who heard about the seminar first. It holds for five days, then moves to the standard price.
