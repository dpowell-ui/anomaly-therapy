Where we stand

A multi-facted approach customized for you.

Anomaly is a boutique therapy and consultancy practice in Nashville for high-performing individuals, couples, founders, executives, creatives, and athletes seeking emotional regulation, relational depth, clarity, and sustainable success.

The room is the relief. Anomaly is the moment the armor comes off — met with intelligence rather than sentiment.

Who we work with

Nashville professionals who are high-performing and high-earning, music-industry humans navigating the imbalance of their field, founders and executives carrying invisible weight, and people seeking refuge and confidence in their identity, relationships, and relational histories.
