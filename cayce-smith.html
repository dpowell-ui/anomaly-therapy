In the room

How Cayce works.

Cayce works with high achieving professionals, creatives, and couples who are used to holding everything together, even when it comes at a personal cost.

She helps clients step out of patterns of overfunctioning and reconnect with themselves and the people they love, in a way that feels intentional and sustainable.

Outside the room

The rest of her.

A Nashville native, Cayce returned home with a deep appreciation for the community that helped shape her. Before becoming a therapist, she built a career in public relations, but over time felt called toward work centered on meaningful human connection. Equal parts curious and courageous, she enrolled in yoga teacher training just before beginning graduate school, a decision that unexpectedly led her to Jess and Anomaly Therapy years before officially joining the team. Outside the therapy room you will find Cayce searching for Nashville's best matcha, practicing yoga, planning ambitious hikes with friends, or spending time with her husband and their Mini Australian Shepherd, Margot.
