In the room

How Jillee works.

Through Anomaly Therapy's Accessible Care Program, Jillee is passionate about creating a welcoming, inclusive space where clients feel seen, supported, and empowered to show up authentically.

She believes healing happens through meaningful connection and tailors her approach to each client's unique needs. By honoring the relationship between the mind and body, Jillee partners with clients to foster greater self-awareness, resilience, and lasting growth.

Outside the room

The rest of her.

New to Nashville, Jillee enjoys exploring the city and finding the places that make it feel like home. Outside the therapy room, you'll find her hiking, practicing yoga, perfecting her espresso skills, or spending time outdoors with family, friends, and her faith community.
