Built for a life in music

Nashville runs on music, and the people who work within the industry carry a unique set of pressures and responsibilities.

At Anomaly, therapy works around that life rather than against it. We see people in our office on Patterson Street and virtually anywhere in Tennessee, so care can continue between rehearsals, writes, meetings, sessions, and time on the road.

What we help with

The weight of a
creative career.

  • Tour life, time away, and coming home to a life that kept moving without you.
  • Performance anxiety and industry pressure.
  • Identity, when worth feels tied to streams, charts, and what you have – or have not – accomplished.
  • Relationships under irregular schedules and public attention.
  • Burnout from navigating the industry’s ups and downs, late nights, demanding schedules, and chasing your dream.
  • Balancing the demands of keeping the business side of the industry moving while supporting the creative work that drives it.

How it works

Therapy at
Anomaly.

Private · Discreet · In person or virtual

We begin with a 90 minute intake to understand your history and what brought you in, then continue with 50 minute sessions.

This is long-form therapy, not a quick fix. We pay attention to regulation, relationships, and what it takes to keep doing work you love without letting it cost you everything else.

Common questions

Music industry therapy, answered.

Is what I share confidential?
Yes. What you share in therapy stays private, within the limits the law sets, and we are used to working with people whose names are known. Our FAQs explain those limits in plain language.

Can we keep working while I’m on tour?
We are licensed in Tennessee, so virtual sessions happen while you are in Tennessee. We plan around tour dates together, so the work can pick up when you are home.

Do I need to be a full-time musician?
No. We work with people across the industry and at every stage of a career, whether you play, write, manage, produce, or work behind the scenes.

What if cost is a barrier?
Our Accessible Care program offers full-length sessions from $50 to $100 with supervised clinical interns. Same office, same standards.
