In the room

How Jess works.

Jess works primarily with executives, high-performing professionals, and individuals in public-facing roles, as well as couples navigating the tension that can arise when ambition, identity, and life outside the home begin to shape the relationship.

She helps clients move beyond old ways of operating, creating space to live and relate with greater clarity, intention, and authenticity.

Outside the room

The rest of her.

Jess is a native New Yorker who grew up on a dairy farm, an unconventional beginning that taught her early that there is rarely just one way to live a meaningful life. While her heart will always belong to New York, she happily traded snowy winters for life in the South. After spending much of her twenties traveling the world, Jess put down roots in Nashville over a decade ago, where she built both her family and Anomaly Therapy. Outside the therapy room, you'll find her at CrossFit, occasionally rolling out a yoga mat, or making the most of every free moment with her husband and daughter.
