Starting the conversation

You don't need the perfect words.

The people we work with are often the ones everyone else leans on. They hold it together at work and at home, which can make it hard for them to say they need support, and hard for you to bring it up.

  1. Choose a private, unhurried moment.

    Not in the middle of a conflict, and not in front of others.

  2. Lead with what you've noticed and that you care.

    "You've seemed stretched thin lately, and I've been thinking about you" lands better than advice.

  3. Offer, then let them decide.

    Share this page or a name, and leave the next step with them. Reaching out on their own terms is part of what makes therapy work.

What happens when they reach out

A simple first step, with no pressure.

First step
A complimentary 15-minute consult to talk through what's going on and whether we're the right fit.
Matching
We match them with a clinician based on fit and availability.
Where
In person at our Nashville office, or virtually anywhere in Tennessee.
Who we see
Adults and couples carrying pressure, visibility and responsibility, and their partners.
Fees
Private pay, $175–$250 a session. We provide superbills for out-of-network reimbursement.
Accessible Care
Intern-led sessions under licensed supervision, $50–$100 based on household income.

About privacy. Therapy is confidential. We can't confirm whether someone has contacted us or is working with us. If you don't hear from us, that's why. It isn't a reflection of you.

For professionals

Physicians, therapists, coaches, attorneys and HR leaders.

If you work with someone who needs a steady, private place to land, we'd be happy to help. We're a strong fit for founders, executives, creatives and other high-functioning adults, and for couples under strain.
