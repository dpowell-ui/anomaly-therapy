You know you want something different. But you keep waiting until you feel ready to do something about it.

You think it through. You make another plan. You ask for another opinion. You tell yourself you just need a little more clarity.

Does this sound familiar? Gosh, so same!

Here's what I want you to consider: You're trying to think your way into a level of confidence that only comes from moving.

Think about how many times you've done something you were scared of, figured it out along the way, and come out the other side thinking, Why did I make that so much bigger in my head?

You didn't need more time to think. You needed evidence that you could handle it.

And you got that evidence by doing the thing but we often overlook the good from decisions we have already made.

Let me be clear, I'm not telling you to throw caution to the wind or make every decision on impulse. Some decisions deserve time and consideration. But there's a point where preparation stops being productive and starts becoming a very convincing way to avoid the discomfort of taking action.

You know the difference. You can feel it when you're going over the same ground for the fifth time, hoping the answer will suddenly feel different. Stop negotiating with yourself.

Ask yourself what you actually need to know to make this decision. Get that information. Then decide.

You don't need to know exactly how it's all going to work out (and I know personally how this can sound and feel challenging and counterproductive). You need to trust yourself enough to respond to what happens next.

That's how self-trust grows.

Not by getting every decision right, but by becoming someone who is willing to make a decision.

Clarity comes through movement, not before it.

So here's your challenge:

Think of the decision you've been circling. The conversation you've been putting off. The thing you keep saying you'll do when the timing feels better.

What is one move you can make in the next 24 hours?

Make it. Then hit reply and tell me what you've been putting off because I absolutely want to celebrate that momentum you have just created for yourself.

You don't have to have the whole path figured out.

You just have to stop waiting for permission to take the first step.

Take gentle care,

JM
