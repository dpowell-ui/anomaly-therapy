07 — Careers Clinicians · Interns · Operations

Practice with us

A small team, chosen carefully.

Anomaly is a boutique practice. We grow slowly and we hire for fit, not headcount. If our model resonates — restrained, rigorous, relationally serious — we'd be glad to hear from you.

01 — Internship

Accessible Care interns.

Anomaly's internship is the entry point for advanced master's students and pre-licensed clinicians who want to learn inside a high-touch private practice. Interns carry their own caseload at a reduced fee, receive weekly supervision from our licensed team, and work in the same office, on the same model, as our senior clinicians.

Applications are reviewed seasonally. Send a CV, a short statement of interest, and your school's practicum requirements.

Apply

02 — Associate

Associate-licensed clinicians.

We periodically bring on associate-licensed therapists pursuing full licensure. Associate roles include weekly supervision toward licensure hours, a clear caseload structure, and competitive private-pay split. Tennessee licensure required.

If we're not actively hiring, we still welcome introductions — most of our team came in this way.

Introduce yourself

03 — Fully Licensed

Fully-licensed therapists.

For licensed clinicians (LPC-MHSP, LCSW, LMFT, PsyD) drawn to private-pay, relationally serious work with high-functioning adults and couples. Flexible schedule, full administrative support, and a referral pipeline of high-quality clients seeking the kind of work we do.

Roles open by referral and conversation rather than posting. The best way in is an email and a 30-minute call.

Email Jess

04 — Operations

Practice operations + client experience.

Occasional openings for client-facing operations roles — scheduling, intake, billing, and the small details that make this place feel cared for. If you've run a boutique practice, hospitality business, or front-of-house in design or fashion, we'd love to talk.

Get in touch

What we look for

Clinical seriousness. Emotional precision. A strong nervous system. The taste to keep things quiet.

We hire slowly and we keep our team small. We're not the right home for every clinician — and that's the point. If our brand voice and our model resonate, the conversation is usually quick.
