Clinicians · Interns

Work with us

A small team, chosen carefully.

Anomaly is a boutique practice. We grow slowly and we hire for fit, not headcount. If our model resonates — restrained, rigorous, relationally serious — we'd be glad to hear from you.

Internship

Accessible Care interns.

Anomaly's internship is the entry point for advanced master-level students who want to learn inside a high-touch private practice. Interns carry their own caseload at a reduced fee, receive weekly supervision from our licensed team, and work in the same office, on the same model, as our senior clinicians.

Applications are reviewed seasonally. Send a CV, a short statement of interest, and your school's practicum requirements.

Apply

Associate

Associate-licensed clinicians.

We periodically bring on associate-licensed therapists pursuing full licensure. Associate roles include weekly team supervision, a clear caseload structure, and competitive pay. Must be working toward Tennessee licensure.

If we're not actively hiring, we still welcome introductions — most of our team came in this way.

Fully Licensed

Flexible schedule, administrative support, and a referral pipeline of high-quality clients seeking the kind of work we do.

What we look for
