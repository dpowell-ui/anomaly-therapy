02 — Services Page 02

Three ways in

One practice.
Three doors.

We operate three distinct offerings — built around the different ways people find their way to support. Each is held to the same standard of care, intellect, and discretion.

i. Talk Therapy PAGE 03

Pillar i.

Traditional
talk therapy.

Individual · Couples · In-person & Virtual

Long-form therapy for high-functioning adults and couples whose ambition, visibility, and complexity have outpaced the language they were given to talk about them.

Our licensed clinicians work with founders, executives, creatives, athletes, and music industry professionals — and the partners they love. Sessions are 50 minutes for individuals and 60 minutes for couples, weekly or biweekly, depending on what your life can hold.

Request an Appointment Ask a question

Specialties & approaches

Relationship & Couples Therapy

For partners navigating attachment, communication, repair, or the relational strain of high-visibility lives. In-person and virtual.

Anxiety, High-Functioning Anxiety & Burnout

For ambitious adults whose nervous systems are running the show. We treat the underlying regulation, not just the symptoms.

Trauma, Complex Trauma & EMDR

EMDR and relational trauma work for adults integrating significant or layered experiences — at a pace that respects the system.

Perinatal & Postpartum Mental Health

Support through fertility, pregnancy, postpartum, loss, and the identity shifts that come with becoming a parent.

Identity, Attachment & Relational Histories

For clients revisiting the relational architecture they were given — choosing what to keep, what to update, what to set down.

Music Industry & Entertainment Professionals

For artists, executives, and crew navigating the imbalances of their field — tour life, public-facing work, and the long arc of a creative career.

ii. Accessible Care PAGE 04

Pillar ii.

Care for less,
without less care.

$85 standard · as low as $30 income-based

Our Accessible Care program is intern-led therapy under direct, ongoing supervision by our licensed clinicians. It exists because we believe cost should not decide who gets the support they need.

Same office. Same standards. Same depth of relational work. The fee is what makes it different.

iii. Workplace Wellness PAGE 05

Pillar iii.

Performance
that holds.

Consulting · Teams · Leadership

Engagements typically run 6–12 weeks · custom scopes available

We work with companies, founders, and leadership teams who want sustainable performance — not burnout dressed up as ambition.

Engagements are scoped to the organization. They might include leadership coaching, team mental-health workshops, founder support, conflict and communication consulting, or an embedded clinician for a season of growth or change.

Engagements include

  • Founder & executive 1:1 support
  • Team workshops on regulation, communication, and conflict
  • Leadership coaching grounded in psychological depth
  • Embedded clinician retainers for growth-stage companies
  • Off-site facilitation and team offsites
  • Custom programming for music-industry organizations
