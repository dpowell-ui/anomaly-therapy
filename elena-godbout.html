In the room

How Elena works.

Elena sees clients through the Accessible Care program at Anomaly. She believes each person already holds inherent strengths, and she approaches therapy with curiosity, patience, and genuine care.

She works with clients to deepen their self understanding, explore new perspectives, and move toward meaningful, lasting growth.

Outside the room

The rest of her.

Originally from Vermont, Elena headed South for college and quickly fell in love with Nashville, deciding to make it home after graduation. She is currently pursuing her Master's in Clinical Mental Health Counseling and completing her clinical internship at Anomaly. Naturally curious, she enjoys exploring new places, trying local restaurants, and embracing new experiences. Outside of Anomaly you will likely find her at a yoga class, reading a good book, traveling somewhere new, or home with her two cats. Above all, she values meaningful connection with the people, and animals, she loves.
