Chattanooga · Virtual

Therapy in Chattanooga,
for people who carry a lot.

Anomaly is a Nashville practice offering virtual therapy to clients across Tennessee, Chattanooga included. The same clinicians, the same standard of care, from wherever you happen to be.

Book a consult

How we serve Chattanooga

Serving Chattanooga,
virtually.

You do not have to drive across the state to work with us. Every Anomaly clinician is licensed throughout Tennessee, so we can see Chattanooga clients over secure video, on the same schedule and with the same attention we bring to the room in Nashville.

For now, our work in Chattanooga is fully virtual. That is not a lesser version of therapy. For most of the people we work with — professionals with full calendars and little margin — it is the version that actually fits a life.

Who we work with

Who we tend
to work with.

Founders and executives carrying more than they let on. Creatives and professionals who look fine on paper and feel stretched underneath it. Couples who are tired of having the same argument. New parents finding their footing in the perinatal season. People sitting with anxiety, burnout, and the quiet weight of holding everything together.

If any of that lands, Chattanooga or not, we should talk.

What we help with

The rooms we spend the most time in.

Anxiety and burnout. Relationship and couples therapy. Perinatal and postpartum support. Trauma. And the particular pressure that comes with being the person everyone else relies on.

Anxiety & Burnout

For ambitious adults whose nervous systems are running the show. We treat the underlying regulation, not just the symptoms.

Relationship & Couples Therapy

For partners navigating attachment, communication, repair, or the relational strain of high-visibility lives.

Perinatal & Postpartum

Support through fertility, pregnancy, postpartum, loss, and the identity shifts that come with becoming a parent.

Trauma

EMDR and relational trauma work for adults integrating significant or layered experiences — at a pace that respects the system.

How it works

How virtual therapy works.

Sessions happen over a private, secure video platform. You get the same full session, the same clinician each week, and none of the commute.

All you need is a quiet spot and a decent connection. We take care of the rest.

Why Anomaly

Small on purpose.

Most directories bury you in a list and leave you to guess. We built something quieter and more deliberate.

Anomaly is small on purpose. Refined, attuned, and straight with you. You are not a slot to fill on a calendar. You are a person we take the time to actually know.

Accessible Care

If cost is
the barrier.

Our Accessible Care program offers full sessions with a supervised clinician on a sliding scale based on household income and need.

The same standard of care, at a lower cost, and open to Chattanooga clients too.

Long view

Growing roots
in Chattanooga.

Right now we serve Chattanooga virtually, and that is honestly the whole offer today. But we are building here on purpose.

As the community grows, so will what we can offer, including in-person options down the road. If you want to be part of that early, we would love to know you.
