Burnout & leadership isolation

When leading
gets lonely.

Burnout in leadership rarely looks like falling apart. More often it looks like:

  • Running on discipline long after the motivation ran out.
  • Sleeping, but never really resting.
  • Being surrounded by people and still alone with the hard decisions.
  • Bringing home less of yourself than you’d like.

None of this means something is wrong with you. It usually means you’ve been carrying a lot, for a long time, with nowhere to set it down.

How it works

How we work
with leaders.

Private · Discreet · In person or virtual

Private by design. We’re private pay, so nothing is billed to an insurer unless you choose to submit a superbill yourself. What you share stays in the room.

In person or by secure video. Meet at our Patterson Street office in Nashville, or by video from anywhere in Tennessee. You can switch as your schedule changes.

Depth, not just coping tips. We look at what sits underneath the pressure: how you relate to work, to control, and to the people closest to you. That’s where the real work is.

Care that respects your time. Work begins with a 90-minute intake, then subsequent 50-minute sessions.
