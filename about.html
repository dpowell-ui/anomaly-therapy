Where we stand

A boutique practice
for the people who
carry a lot.

Anomaly is a private therapy and consultancy practice in Nashville, founded on the belief that high performers, founders, creatives, and the partners they love deserve relational depth — not wellness clichés.

01 · Practice PAGE 03

Who we are

Restraint is the gesture.

Anomaly Therapy was founded by Jess Mansell, LPC-MHSP, to create a different kind of therapy room — one designed for clients whose lives don't slow down, whose visibility shapes their work, and whose ambition has often outpaced their relational vocabulary.

The work is grounded, concise, and emotionally precise. We hold space without performing safety. We challenge without sentimentality. We support without softening reality. This is therapy designed for lives with pressure, complexity, and responsibility — and the relational architecture they require.

We are

  • Discerning
  • Emotionally intelligent
  • Spacious
  • Architectural
  • Modern, refined, private

We are not

  • Trendy wellness
  • Overly clinical
  • Performative or vague
  • Self-help driven
  • Corporate

Meet the team

Clinicians for complex lives.

Our team is intentionally small. Every clinician at Anomaly is selected for their clinical excellence, emotional precision, and ability to sit with the kind of clients who don't usually let anyone in.
